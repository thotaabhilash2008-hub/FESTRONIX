5. DYNAMIC DEPARTMENT MANAGEMENT (SUPER ADMIN)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

* Create New Engineering Departments (Code, Name, Description, Chief Coordinator, Contact, Icon)
* Edit Existing Departments (Update title, code, chief, contact channel, or icon)
* Delete / Remove Departments (Removes from performance matrix, event filters, and login views)
* Auto-Credentialing: When adding a department with a Chief Coordinator, official HOD credentials
  are automatically provisioned (e.g. `hod_aiml` / `aiml2026`).

Location in Portal:
* Go to /#admin -> Sign in as Super Admin (e.g. `dean_sac` / `deansac2026`)
* Tab 1: Engineering Department Performance Matrix
* Click "+ Create Department" or click "Edit" / "Delete" on any department card.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  - Fields: Full Name, Designation, Username, Password, Email, Phone
  - Example username: `finance_desk_1` / Password: `finance2026`
* Edit Existing Payment Users:
  - Click "Edit" on the payment reviewer card in Tab 2 or Tab 4
  - Change username, password, email, phone, designation anytime
* Delete / Revoke Payment Users:
  - Click "Delete" on the payment reviewer card in Tab 2 or Tab 4 to permanently deny portal access
* How Payment Users Log In:
  - Go to /#admin -> Tab 3: "3. FINANCE & CHECK-IN"
  - Enter Username (e.g. `finance_reviewer` or `finance_ramesh`)
  - Enter Passcode (e.g. `finance2026`)
  - Access is scoped to the Payment Verification Desk to approve/reject UTRs.

7. EVENT COORDINATORS MANAGEMENT & DELETION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Super Admins can dynamically:
* Create New Event Coordinators:
  - Go to /#admin -> Tab 4: "Official Coordinator Registry & Appointments"
  - Click "+ Create Event Coordinator" or "+ Appoint Coordinator"
  - Role: "🎯 Event Operations Coordinator" or "⚡ Faculty Event Convener"
* Delete Event Coordinators:
  - Locate any coordinator card in Tab 4
  - Click the red "Delete Coordinator" button at the bottom of the card, or the "Delete" button in the top right badge row
  - Confirm the prompt: their access is permanently revoked and purged from the active registry immediately.
